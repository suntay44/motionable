## Motion rules

1. Headlines left-aligned and big, revealed line by line from a mask; only the end card is centred.
2. Hard cuts only on the beat. Morphs 0.25–0.45 s in-out; entrances ease out, sometimes with overshoot.
3. Camera: slow push-in per scene, eased back on each morph; a tiny pulse on every beat; shake only on stamps and impacts.
4. Colour carries the story: <!-- list the colour sequence -->
5. The last 1–2 s hold perfectly still.

## Bans

- No centred headline on a gradient (except the end card).
- No feature the product doesn't have; every UI element is a real screenshot or redrawn from one.
- No tilted, covered or "bursting out of the screen" phone images (Apple's marketing rules).
- No text in the bottom 20 % or right 12 % (social app buttons).
- Store badges follow rules.md: Apple's badge never animates; Google's artwork only for apps on Google Play.
