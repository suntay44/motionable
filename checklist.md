# motionable critique checklist

Render a contact sheet with two or three stills per scene: one mid-entrance, one settled, one mid-transition. Look at it, then score each category from 1 to 10. **Fix anything below 8**, re-render the sheet, and score again. Stop when everything is 8 or higher, or after three rounds. Then tell the user what's still weak.

| Category | 8+ means |
|---|---|
| **Hook** | Frame 1 already shows bold type or an image; by 1 s the viewer knows it's about something specific |
| **Readability** | Every headline is fully on screen for at least ~1.2 s; nothing overlaps; no text in the safe zones; contrast holds on every background |
| **Story** | Each scene shows one real benefit; the order builds; the end card says what it is and where to get it |
| **Motion** | Something moves on every beat; morphs connect scenes instead of hard-cutting between unrelated slides; nothing jitters or pops |
| **Brand** | Real colours, logo and screenshots; type matches the product; looks like *this* product, not a template |
| **Rules** | Everything in rules.md holds: claims verified, badges untouched, phones upright, final hold still |
| **Sound** | The audio levels print sanely: muffled stretches read quieter than just before; effects line up with `T` times; no hit without picture |

Also check:
- Mid-transition stills: shapes and text shouldn't look broken or collide in an ugly way. Briefly odd is fine; a mess is not.
- The final hold frame: is it a good thumbnail on its own?
- The longest line at the auto-fit size: is it still big enough?
