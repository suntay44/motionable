## Story

- **Promise:** cooking for a crowd stops being maths.
- **Tension → payoff:** "Cooking for 40?" → Ponda scales it and prices it.
- **Signature moment:** on the real Scaling screen, servings roll 4 → 40 and the total $12.40 → $124.00, while $3.10 per pax holds still.
- **Hook candidates:**
  1. "Cooking for 40?" (problem, with plates multiplying)
  2. "What does one plate cost?" (question)
  3. "Scale any recipe." (promise)

  **Chosen: 1.** Problem hooks lead software-app ad spend (RESEARCH.md), and the plates make the problem visible.
- **Arc:** hook → scale (signature) → cost per plate → one list → proof chips → end card.
