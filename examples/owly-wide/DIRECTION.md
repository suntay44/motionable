## Story

- **Promise:** your family's to-dos, shared without the nagging.
- **Tension → payoff:** reminders by text get ignored → the list lives on their Home Screen.
- **Signature moment, on the app's own recordings:** Mom taps +, types "Pack gym clothes" (the camera zooms in on her typing) and taps Add. The task flies to Leo's phone, where his recording shows him tick that same task on his widget (0/3 → 1/3). Then "Done by Leo: Pack gym clothes" lands on Mom's phone and her list ticks too.
- **Hook candidates:**
  1. "Still texting reminders?" (problem)
  2. "Who's doing what this week?" (question)
  3. "A list the whole family shares." (promise)

  **Chosen: 1.** It's the most relatable problem, and the nag ("Piano at 4!" · "Bins out tonight!" · "Hello??") is shown as chat bubbles.
