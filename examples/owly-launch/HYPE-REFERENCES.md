## Diagnosis of current Owly film

Repeated headline-above-one-screen composition; long settled holds; only one strongly dramatized interaction. Master capacity section holds almost8seconds. Moving existing cuts earlier kept information density low. Beat alignment alone does not create visual variety or escalating product discovery.

## Historical proposal (implemented and refined in Film.swift)

Brisk, playful, information-rich Owly launch film. Keep green/cream and the hook. Use larger product crops, split parent/child phones, lifted task chips, drawn arrows and owl punctuation. Each supporting movement should explain an actual feature. Design around one main readable idea per moment, with secondary visual motion.

30s sequence:
0–3 You write it / They tick it: words and task chips snap into two linked phone layouts.
3–7 Type naturally: real Practice piano4pm entry; visually isolate text→recognized time chip→finished task.
7–12 Share with family: real parent list→child widget, task card handoff, visible measured tick and0/3→1/3 count.
12–17 Routines: show verified real daily routine UI and progress, using assets from the product. Require evidence selection before scene code.
17–22 Plan a day that fits: actual capacity bar and duration labels; punch in on load→overage→surrounding tasks. Avoid implying an unrecorded feature operation.
22–26 Rapid product recap using already explained shared lists/widget/routines/capacity, no new paragraph or claim.
26–30 Owl identity and Live on the App Store; final2s still.

15s cut: hook→natural entry→shared handoff→widget completion→fast recap/end. Two main benefits, enough result reading time.

Motion targets are creative proposals, not research-derived engagement guarantees: meaningful visual change roughly every0.5–1s during energetic passages; explanatory beats2–4s; alternate full-screen type, macro UI and split layouts;1–1.5s readable proof pauses. Keep music/effects only. Stronger percussion and bass, a short drop immediately before tick, layered click/ding/re-entry payoff. Select tempo to support actual recording readability rather than speeding every clip indiscriminately.
